How It Works

From the aircraft to your portal — here's how FlightLevel Technologies turns raw signals into actionable insights.

1. Antenna Receives

Ground antenna picks up the aircraft's ADS-B broadcast in real time

2. Camera Tracks

Camera locks onto aircraft and records operation

3. System Analyzes

Software decodes signal, processes data, matches operation to recording, and displays data in the user portal
