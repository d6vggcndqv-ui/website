Reports

Generate any of the airport's reports as a PDF or spreadsheet (CSV). Pick the date or period, then press the button.

Demonstration data. Aircraft movements are real ADS-B tracks recorded at Hudson Valley Regional Airport (POU). Noise levels (October 19–25, 2026) are simulated at seven virtual monitors and evaluated against Torrance Municipal Airport's published limits. Violation flags are candidates for staff review, not findings. Owner contact details in violation letters are sample data.
Noise monitoring & enforcement

Daily Verified Violation List PDFCSV

Every candidate violation for one day, by N-number: noise over the Lmax or SENEL limit, curfew departures, and training operations. Each row has a box for staff verification.

Monthly / Quarterly Noise Report PDF

CNEL by monitor against the 65 dB benchmark, noise events and violations by type, runway operations, the aircraft with the most violations, and the calibration record.

Hearing Board Case Packet PDF

For one noise event at least 1.5 dB over a limit: the noise curve, the flight track past the monitor, how the operation was classified, the monitor's calibration that day, and the aircraft's other events.

Violation Letter PDF

A draft notice to the aircraft's registered owner for one violation, filled in with the event details, ready for staff review before it is sent.

Violation Candidates CSV

The same export as the Noise Monitoring page: every noise exceedance event and every flagged runway operation for a date range, one row each.

Airport operations

Operations Summary PDF

The Dashboard report: total operations, takeoffs, landings and touch-and-gos, with operations by day, hour, runway, class and AAC/ADG.

Flight History CSV

The Flight History export: every logged operation with date, time, registration, operation, runway, AAC/ADG, category and class.

Flight Track Report PDF

The Noise Abatement report for one flight: aircraft, times, altitude and speed, and the flight path on a map colored by altitude.

Landing Fees CSV

Transient landings with fee tier and amount owed. To set up this report with your fee schedule contact will@fl-technologies.com
