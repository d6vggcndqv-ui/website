Noise Monitoring

Demonstration data. Aircraft movements are real ADS-B tracks recorded at Hudson Valley Regional Airport (POU), October 19–25, 2026. Noise levels are simulated at seven virtual monitors and evaluated against Torrance Municipal Airport's published limits (82 dBA Lmax / 88 dB SENEL; 76 / 82 during the departure curfew). Flags are candidates for staff review, not findings.

Select an operation to see how it was classified:
its height and speed over the runway, and its path on the map.
Each operation is classified from the aircraft's recorded track. Low approach: never touches the runway. Stop-and-go: comes to a complete stop on the runway, then takes off from that stop. Taxi-back: lands and taxis without parking, then takes off again. Touch-and-go: touches down and takes off again in any other way. Torrance: touch-and-gos and stop-and-gos are prohibited at all times; taxi-backs and low approaches are permitted 10 AM–6 PM, Monday–Friday. Operations the track can't settle are marked Needs review. Helicopter operations are not shown here.
